Privacy Policy for Winter Fishing Master

Martin Zub respects user privacy and aims to keep data use simple and transparent. Winter Fishing Master may use limited analytics and advertising technologies to improve the app, understand app performance, and support advertising campaigns.

1. Data Collection

Winter Fishing Master does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contact lists, precise location data, or other directly provided personal information.

The app may collect limited technical and usage data through Firebase Analytics. This may include app events, session information, device type, operating system version, app version, approximate region, and other diagnostic or performance-related information.

2. Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used, how stable it is, and how its features perform. This information helps us improve gameplay, fix issues, and make better product decisions.

We do not use Firebase Analytics data to personally identify individual users. Analytics data is used only for product improvement, performance analysis, and app development purposes.

3. Advertising SDK and Campaign Measurement

The app may include the Appsflire advertising SDK to support advertising, attribution, and campaign performance measurement for platforms such as Google Ads and Facebook.

Advertising and attribution technologies may process technical information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, app interaction events, install data, ad interaction data, and similar information needed to display ads, measure campaign performance, and prevent fraud.

4. How We Use Data

  • To analyze app performance and stability.
  • To understand which features are useful to players.
  • To improve gameplay, usability, and product quality.
  • To measure advertising campaign effectiveness.
  • To support advertising through third-party platforms where applicable.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell user data. We also do not manually transfer user data to third parties for independent use.

However, third-party SDK providers such as Firebase, AppsFlyer, Google Ads, or Facebook-related advertising services may process limited technical and analytics data as necessary to provide analytics, advertising, attribution, measurement, and related services.

6. Data Storage

We do not store personal user profiles on our own servers. Analytics and advertising-related information may be stored and processed by the third-party services used in the app according to their own data processing and retention practices.

7. Children’s Privacy

Winter Fishing Master is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that personal information from a child has been collected without appropriate consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete or disable such information where possible.

8. User Choices

Users may be able to limit advertising tracking or reset advertising identifiers through their device settings. Availability of these controls depends on the operating system and device settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app’s functionality, analytics setup, advertising SDKs, or privacy practices change. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
